# PLSC 508 (Fall 2026) — Application Reading Review Sign-ups

Write a review of, and lead discussion for, one of the week's application papers. When you claim a slot you must state which reading you are reviewing; it appears on the sheet next to your name. One readings slot per student. First come, first served.

**Sign up only for a second slot (Slot 2) if Slot 1 on the respective day is already full.** The bot enforces this automatically: your claim goes into Slot 1 if it is open, and into Slot 2 only when Slot 1 for that day is taken.

**To claim a slot:** use the sign-up page at **https://brucedesmarais.com/plsc508signup/** — no account of any kind is needed. Pick your week, enter your name, and the reading you will review, and submit; your claim is timestamped the moment you submit, shows as pending immediately, and lands on this sheet within about ten minutes. Claims are honored strictly in the order received, and Slot 2 of any week opens only after every week's Slot 1 is filled. (If you prefer, and have a GitHub account, the [issue claim form](../../issues/new?template=claim-readings.yml) also works.)
